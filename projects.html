01 Jun. 2025 - Oct. 2025

Vision-Language Models-based Autonomous Multi-robot Planning

Visual Foundation Models Vision-Language Models Motion Planning
  • Built a zero-shot perception-reasoning-planning pipeline that converts natural-language instructions and raw visual inputs into executable multi-robot plans.

02 Feb. 2025 - Jun. 2025

Diffusion Model-based Policy Learning for Human-to-Robot Object Handover

Imitation Learning Diffusion Policy Human-to-Robot Handover
  • Developed a two-stage imitation learning framework that combines offline demonstration synthesis with diffusion-based handover policy learning.
  • Designed a 4D spatiotemporal flow representation for human-object motion and validated the learned policy on an ABB IRB1600 robot with an 87.5% task success rate.
03 Oct. 2024 - Jan. 2025

Digital Twin-driven Multi-robot Intelligent Construction System

Digital Twin Multi-robot Construction Visual Servoing
  • Developed ConstrucTwin, a digital twin-driven multi-robot framework linking physical ABB robots, Unity3D twins, communication networks, and task-motion planning.
  • Built RGB-D visual servoing, real-time monitoring, 6D pose estimation, and MoveIt 2-based hierarchical planning for robotic construction scenarios.
04 Mar. 2024 - Jun. 2025

Diverse Human Motion Prediction for Human-Robot Collaboration

Human Motion Prediction Collision Avoidance Human-Robot Collaboration
  • Developed FineMLD, a fine-grained motion latent diffusion framework for diverse and plausible human motion prediction in shared workspaces.
  • Collected 75,541 synchronized human-robot motion frames on an ABB IRB1600 HRC platform and integrated prediction into closed-loop collision avoidance.
